An Evening Among the Lights

Celebrate the season in true holiday style with a horse-drawn carriage or sleigh ride through the famous Windcrest Christmas Light Up. Our sightseeing rides take you through streets aglow with twinkling displays, festive décor, and neighborhood cheer, ideal for families, couples, and anyone who loves the magic of Christmas lights.

Reservations are strongly recommended to avoid wait times and ensure availability. While walk-up rides are welcome, they may not be available on all nights if we are fully booked.

The Experience

What to Expect

From the lights to the horses, every part of the evening is planned with your comfort in mind.

I.

Windcrest Light Up

Tour through the legendary Windcrest Light Up, known throughout Texas for its spectacular synchronized Christmas light shows and festive decorations that draw visitors from across the region.

II.

First-Ever Sleigh Rides

Be among the first to experience our brand-new one-horse open sleigh, the only horse-drawn sleigh in South Texas, a once-in-a-lifetime addition to your holiday memories.

III.

Gentle Horses

Our beautiful, well-trained horses are gentle and experienced, ensuring a smooth and comfortable ride for guests of all ages.

IV.

Perfect for Everyone

Whether it's a family outing, a romantic date night, or a special celebration, our tours create unforgettable holiday memories for all ages.

V.

Expert Guides

Our knowledgeable guides share stories about Windcrest's holiday traditions and make sure you see the most spectacular displays along the route.

VI.

Photo Opportunities

Capture magical moments against the backdrop of millions of twinkling lights. Every turn offers a photo you'll treasure.
